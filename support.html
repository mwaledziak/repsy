When you report a problem

Include your phone model, Android or iOS version, the Repsy version (Settings screen), and what you were doing when it went wrong. Screenshots help.

Common questions

The timer stops when I lock my phone.

On Android, allow notifications for Repsy so the workout notification can run. Some phone makers also restrict background apps; set Repsy to "Unrestricted" battery usage if the timer pauses.

I can't hear the bell.

Check that the mute button on the timer screen is off, that Master volume in Settings is up, and that your phone isn't on silent.

How do I remove ads or get my data deleted?

Repsy keeps your settings only on your device, so uninstalling the app removes them. See the Privacy Policy for details on ads and crash reports.
