Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Repsy is published by Mobillasoft (Warsaw, Poland). This policy explains what the Repsy app does with your data. Short version: no account, no personal profile, and your timer settings never leave your phone.

What stays on your device

Your timer setup, sound, vibration and appearance choices are stored locally on your device. We don't receive them.

Ads

Repsy shows a banner ad while a workout runs, served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), Repsy asks for your consent through Google's consent form before loading personalized ads. On iOS, you'll also see Apple's tracking prompt and can decline. You can change your choices in your device's ad settings. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Crash reports

Repsy uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports so bugs get fixed. A report can include your device model, OS version, app version, the state of the app when it crashed, and an installation identifier. It does not include your name or contact details. See Firebase privacy information.

Permissions

Repsy asks for notification permission (Android) to show the ongoing workout notification and its controls, vibration for haptic feedback, and the ability to keep the screen awake during a workout. None of these send data anywhere.

What we don't do

We don't ask you to create an account, we don't sell your data, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

Repsy is a general-audience fitness timer and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age).

Your rights

If you're in the EEA, UK or another region with data protection rights, you can ask about, access, correct or delete data we hold about you, and you can complain to your local data protection authority. Because Repsy doesn't hold an account or profile for you, there is usually little to delete, but email us and we'll check. Data held by Google (ads) or Firebase (crash reports) is also subject to Google's own tools and policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
