Repsy is a no-nonsense interval timer for boxing, MMA and any round-based workout. Big numbers you can read from across the room, clear bells, and nothing in your way.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store

What you get

Your rounds, your rules

Set round time, rest time and the number of rounds, or go infinite. Boxing and MMA presets get you started in one tap.

Readable at a glance

Huge countdown, a clear WORK or REST state, and a progress bar for every round. Light and dark appearance.

Sound and haptics

Choose your bell or beep, last-seconds countdown, phase-end sound and vibration. Mute with one tap.

Stays awake

The screen stays on during a workout. On Android, pause and skip from the notification, even with the phone locked.

3-2-1 countdown

Optional lead-in before round one, so you can get your gloves on.

No account

No sign-up and no login. Your settings stay on your device.
